Mascot

The Light

A flat illustrated sun with a dashed orbit: the same sun that rises in every landscape, so the mascot and the art feel like one world. No face, no halo, no wings. In the app: components/Light.tsx (react-native-svg + Reanimated), prop state.

Illustration

Eight landscapes, one per mood

Flat layered vector scenes: sky, sun or moon, mountain ridges, pines, water with light stripes. Generated here from one function (design/kit/kit.js); for the product, export each once as an SVG file and reuse it on cards, players, the paywall and the web.

Colour

Three palettes to compare

Switch Dawn / Easter / Olive in the toolbar. Light theme by default (cream + white cards). Pastels and illustrations look the same in dark mode and keep dark ink on them, like the SAT site.
